How a ZIP campaign works

Step 1

Find your ZIP

We count every kid 18 and under in each ZIP code area using the Census Bureau's American Community Survey, not just babies born 2025–2028.

Step 2

Check eligibility

ZIPs with at least 5,000 kids have their own campaign. Smaller ZIPs team up in an Area Fund with neighbors that are all within 25 miles of each other, until they pass 5,000 kids together.

Step 3

Give and watch it grow

Every gift is added to a running total and split evenly into each child's Trump Account. Want to pick the places yourself? Start a community campaign with its own goal.
